Frequently Asked Questions
Which ProClean services can I book?

Car-care services are currently available for booking. Sofa, mattress, and water tank cleaning are shown as previews and will be enabled after launch.

What should I provide at the time of service?

Customers must provide an alternating current power source and two buckets of water in our container.

What happens if my vehicle gets damaged during the wash?

While we take great care during service, we are not responsible for pre-existing damage. If any damage occurs during our cleaning process, we will investigate and resolve the matter, and where applicable, compensation will be handled as per our liability policy.

Do you clean the interior of the car as well?

Yes. We provide both exterior and interior cleaning, including vacuuming, dusting, wiping surfaces, and cleaning windows. You can also opt for a full detailing package.

Do you offer subscription or membership plans?

Yes, we offer flexible subscription plans for regular customers with discounted rates and priority booking for recurring services. Please contact us for plan details.

Can you clean a car that is extremely dirty or muddy?

Yes. Please inform us in advance. Additional charges may apply depending on the level of dirt if extra time and effort are required.

Do I need to be present during the service?

Your presence is not mandatory, but someone should be available to provide access and any special instructions. If you cannot be there, leave the keys with a trusted person or in a designated area.
